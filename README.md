## 🛠 What I'm building

### Founder Discovery & Deal-Sourcing Engine

I'm experimenting with alternative ways of discovering technical founders **before they enter conventional VC sourcing pipelines**.

I've built Python-based discovery tools that scan public developer ecosystems such as **GitHub and Hugging Face** to identify builders working on problems relevant to my investment themes.

The broader goal is to combine:

**Developer ecosystems → Accelerators & residencies → Universities → Professional networks → Startup communities → Traditional sourcing**

into a repeatable founder-discovery system.

## 🔬 Project Evolution

This project developed iteratively rather than being designed as a single system from the beginning.

### 01 — Company Discovery Experiment

`Comp_scraper.py`

An early attempt at broad company discovery using thesis-related keywords.

The experiment generated a large number of results but also significant noise. It helped identify a core problem with broad keyword-based sourcing: **more results do not necessarily produce better deal flow.**

This version is retained as an early experiment rather than a current sourcing engine.

### 02 — Professional Profile Discovery

`linkedin_finder.py`

An experiment in connecting discovered builders with professional profiles.

The approach added limited incremental value to the sourcing workflow and was not developed further.

### 03 — Source-Specific Founder Discovery

`github_scraper.py` and `hf_scraper.py`

The next iteration shifted the focus from broad company discovery toward identifying **observable builder activity**.

GitHub provided signals from repositories and open-source activity, while Hugging Face provided another discovery surface for AI builders, models and applications.

These engines were subsequently refined with better thesis matching, prioritization and research workflows.

### 04 — Master Founder Discovery Engine

`master_founder_scraper.py`

The latest iteration consolidates learnings from the earlier experiments into a broader discovery engine.

Rather than maximizing the number of profiles collected, the focus increasingly shifted toward:

**signal quality → thesis relevance → prioritization → manual validation**

The master engine represents the current direction of the project and will continue to evolve as additional sourcing channels and feedback loops are tested.
